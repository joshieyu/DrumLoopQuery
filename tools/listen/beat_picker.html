Recording beat picker

Every GMD test-split loop, played twice through , as participants will hear it. Pick beats for each member with the A–D buttons. Picks are saved in this browser; export the CSV to keep them. Aim for a balance of straight and swung, sparse and busy, and skip beats that are unreasonable to beatbox.

Feel comes from where the off-beat hits fall on the 12-steps-per-beat grid: triplet share is the share on triplet steps rather than 16th steps, and you set the swung cutoff. Loose is the share of off-beat hits on neither, i.e. loosely played timing. Density splits the test split into thirds by sounds per loop.
