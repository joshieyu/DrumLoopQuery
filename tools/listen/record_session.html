Beatbox recording session

For each beat: the loop plays twice, then the click counts you in and you beatbox two bars along with it. Wired headphones on, so the click and the loop stay out of the mic. Saved takes appear under each beat; mark each one usable or not.

1. Microphone

Off. Use the built-in laptop mic; the page turns off echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain.

2. Latency

Turn on the mic first.

Unplug the headphones (or hold an earcup against the laptop mic), turn the volume up, press Measure, then plug them back in. Measure again if you change headphones. Bluetooth headphones add a long delay, so use wired ones.
